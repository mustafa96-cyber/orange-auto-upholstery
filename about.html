A neighborhood shop you can trust.

Orange Auto Upholstery is a small Orange shop that rebuilds and recovers interiors for cars, trucks, and boats, walk in or by appointment.

Our approach

Careful work, every time.

Working with interiors every day builds a level of care you can feel. We know the materials, the patterns, and the details because we see them every day.

Because we run walk in or by appointment, your car gets scheduled time on the bench instead of a place in a waiting line. You know when to drop it off, and we know exactly what we are doing that day.

Most of all, we give straight answers. Customers on Yelp say the same thing again and again: fair prices and honest advice. We would rather tell you what a job really needs than sell you more than you want.
